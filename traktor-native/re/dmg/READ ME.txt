Track Kommander inside Traktor
==============================

This disk image makes "Traktor Pro 4 TK": your own Traktor Pro 4, started with Track
Kommander inside it. It is made on your Mac, from your Traktor, and sits BESIDE your
normal Traktor Pro 4, which is not changed: use either one.

  Install Track Kommander.command   makes it and puts it in place (below)
  Traktor Pro 4 folder              a shortcut to where it goes: beside your Traktor Pro 4,
                                    in /Applications/Native Instruments/Traktor Pro 4
  Track Kommander Files             what the installer uses (Track Kommander itself,
                                    with its own Python: nothing else to install)

Needs: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) and a Traktor Pro 4 version Track Kommander
supports (the installer says which, and stops if yours is another; Traktor shows its
version in Help > About). About 700 MB of disk space.


1. Install
----------

Double-click "Install Track Kommander.command". A Terminal window opens and the
installer runs there.

The first time, macOS refuses it ("Apple could not verify ..."): click Done, open
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to the
installer's name, then double-click it again.

It asks for your Mac password once, at the start, in Terminal (Traktor's folder is
protected; nothing shows while you type it). If Traktor is running, it waits for you
to quit it. Any question comes up in Terminal too: answer y or n. An old copy goes to
the Bin, it is never deleted.

Without the Privacy & Security step: open Terminal (Applications > Utilities >
Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

    bash "/Volumes/Track Kommander/Install Track Kommander.command"


2. What the installer does
--------------------------

It finds your Traktor Pro 4 (with Spotlight) and checks it is a supported version.
Then it makes "Traktor Pro 4 TK.app" next to it, in the same folder (usually
/Applications/Native Instruments/Traktor Pro 4/):

  - Traktor's executable, its Apple Silicon part, told to load Track Kommander,
  - Track Kommander and its own Python, inside the app,
  - your Traktor's own Frameworks, PlugIns and Resources, through links (not copied).

The app is signed "ad hoc" for your Mac. A changed Traktor cannot keep Native
Instruments' signature, and Track Kommander has no Apple developer signature of its
own. The installer removes the download's quarantine mark from the app's own files, so
macOS opens it without "Move to Bin".


3. Start it
-----------

The installer opens it the first time. Then open "Traktor Pro 4 TK" (next to Traktor
Pro 4); if your normal Traktor is running, quit it first. Track Kommander appears
inside Traktor (its band and its entry in the browser tree) after a few seconds. The
first start takes longer, and the first analysis of your collection takes a while.

macOS may ask whether "Traktor Pro 4" may use your Music folder, your microphone or
your controllers: allow, as you did for your normal Traktor (macOS sees the TK copy as
a different app, so it asks again).

When Native Instruments updates Traktor, "Traktor Pro 4 TK" stops matching it: install
the Track Kommander release made for the new Traktor version.


4. If something does not work
-----------------------------

Send these files:

    /tmp/tk_dylib.log                                      (Track Kommander's loader)
    ~/Library/Logs/Track Kommander/embed.log               (its start inside Traktor)
    ~/Library/Application Support/Track Kommander/logs/    (Track Kommander's own log)


5. Remove it
------------

Drag "Traktor Pro 4 TK.app" (next to your Traktor Pro 4) to the Bin. Your normal
Traktor Pro 4 and its collection are as before. Track Kommander's own data (its
database of your tracks) is in ~/Library/Application Support/Track Kommander, and its
caches in ~/Library/Caches/Track Kommander: delete those folders too to remove
everything.
